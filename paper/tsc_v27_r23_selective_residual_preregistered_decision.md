# TSC v27/r23 Selective Rigid-Residual Arbitration: Preregistered Decision

Frozen before any v27 target evaluation was launched. The six development
targets may be used only for the aggregate decision below. Salt Lake remains
sealed and must not be read, fitted, scored, or used to choose a gate.

## Motivation

TSC v26/r22 established that a rigid-anchored one-step residual is structurally
safer than replacing the rigid action score. Mobility improved macro normalized
action regret by 3.53%; mobility plus queue improved five of six cities by
3.17%. Neither passed the frozen 10% promotion gate. The remaining failure mode
is action-group selective: the two experts often agree, while a minority of
incorrect residual overrides removes most of the available gain.

## Frozen Method

For each source city, rigid and residual action scores are generated by the
existing whole-city OOF pipeline. Only groups in which their selected actions
differ enter the arbiter training set. A domain-balanced Ridge model predicts

`observed source cost(residual action) - observed source cost(rigid action)`.

Its eight fixed inputs are score-space quantities available in every target:
the two experts' opposing penalties and margins, their corrections at both
selected actions, the correction range, and log action count. Route, network,
city, target context, and target transition labels are excluded.

The arbiter chooses the residual expert only when the predicted cost difference
plus a one-sided source-city OOF error quantile is below zero. Otherwise the
complete action group falls back to rigid. The final arbiter and its error
margin use source cities only. This is a target-label-free deployment rule, not
a target adapter and not a formal distribution-free target-city guarantee.

## Frozen Sensitivity Grid

Two v26 bases are retained:

1. one-step mobility residual;
2. one-step mobility plus queue residual.

For each base, one-sided error quantiles are fixed at 0.50, 0.75, and 0.90.
Ridge alpha is fixed at 10.0; at least 24 source expert-disagreement groups and
three source cities are required. A requested gate that cannot be fitted fails
closed to rigid. No other feature, alpha, threshold, or mechanism combination
may be added after seeing v27 results.

## Development Evaluation

Targets, one per physical node: Grid4x4, Cologne1, Ingolstadt1, Atlanta1x5,
Hangzhou4x4, and Manhattan28x7. Every target is a complete-city holdout with
zero target transition/action labels. The v25 policy-consistent immutable cache,
source-rule baseline, source generator, launch snapshot, result SHA-256, model
protocol, source-domain count, and arbiter diagnostics must pass fail-closed
audit.

The v26 rigid result and matching raw residual results are immutable references;
they are not rerun or silently replaced.

## Frozen Promotion Gate

A family is eligible only if all conditions hold against rigid:

- macro mean normalized action-regret improvement is at least 10%;
- at least four of six development cities improve;
- maximum city regression is at most 0.05;
- every target has zero target label budget and a complete city holdout;
- every enabled arbiter reports five source domains, source-city OOF fitting,
  the frozen quantile/alpha, and `target_labels_used = false`.

Among eligible families, choose lowest macro regret. Within an absolute macro
regret tie of 0.005, prefer the higher error quantile, then mobility only. If no
family passes, Salt Lake remains sealed and v27 is diagnostic only.

## Claim Boundary

Passing v27 permits one Salt Lake confirmation run; it does not establish
closed-loop superiority. Failing v27 rejects this arbiter as the promoted main
method and requires a new preregistered development-stage mechanism.
