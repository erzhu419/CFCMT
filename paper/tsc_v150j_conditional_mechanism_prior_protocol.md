# TSC V150J Conditional Mechanism-Prior Protocol

## Hypothesis

V150I made B100 transfer non-degrading but identified a useful source only for
Atlanta. V150J tests the diagnosis that one state conditioner per mechanism
cannot distinguish when similar aggregate pressure states have different local
service responses because of right-of-way and neighboring signal execution.

## Single Representation Change

The design remains deterministic and deployment-observable. Queue, spillback
and mobility blocks retain their V150I conditioner. Execution additionally
interacts action features with neighboring switching and clearance. Network
propagation additionally uses neighboring protected/permissive service and
switching. Right-of-way action features additionally interact with neighboring
protected/permissive service, elapsed green, switching and clearance.

These features enter only the mechanism parameter prior. They do not enter the
rigid residual parent set, use future outcomes, or alter the exact source-null
fallback. The source-prior strength stays at the V150I B100 value `0.025`.

## Gate

The B100 groups, common untouched evaluation set, 36 candidates, full-selector
crossfit, matched placebo and city-level gate are unchanged. At least two cities
must admit a source, no city may regress rigid CFCMT, and each admitted city must
improve both rigid and its same-candidate placebo. Passing authorizes a
closed-loop development experiment only.
